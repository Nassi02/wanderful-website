What kind of website do you need?

The starting point

A clear, fast website that brings in enquiries

Your services, your prices or the areas you cover, proof, a way to contact you in two clicks. With subtle animation that guides the reader. That's what most SMEs need, and it's already far above average.

Going further

A memorable website, with 3D and interaction

A product you can spin, a configurator, a scene that reacts to the mouse: this makes sense when the object or the experience is what sells. It then takes quality visuals or 3D models, and we make sure the site's speed doesn't suffer.

Not as a first step

Your offer isn't clear yet

If you can't yet say in one sentence what you offer and to whom, a new website won't solve the problem. In that case we start with strategy and messaging, and the website then brings them to life.

At the first conversation we tell you which level of website fits your business and your budget. A spectacular website that generates no enquiries is of no use to you: the effect must always serve the message.

Planning a new website or a redesign? The easiest thing is to talk: a first conversation is enough to find out what you really need.

Our approach

The visit→The message→The spark→The enquiry

A beautiful website that generates no enquiries is just a brochure. We start from what visitors need to understand in a few seconds, then what should convince them, and finally what makes them take action.

Animation and 3D come next, in service of that journey: drawing the eye to the right information, making a product tangible, making people want to stay. Every effect needs a reason to exist, and must never slow the page down.

The website you're on is the example: animated icons, interactive demos, an English version, a built-in assistant, all made in-house. Frequently asked questions

Do 3D and animation slow the website down?

Not if they're done well. 3D elements only load when they come on screen, a lighter version is served on mobile, and an image replaces them if the device doesn't support them.

Animations also respect the visitor's "reduce motion" device setting. And we measure the speed of every page before launch.

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the number of pages, languages and content to produce. The timeline is set out in writing with the quote, step by step, after the first conversation.

What most often slows a project down is copy and photos: we can take care of them so the website doesn't get stuck.

Will I be able to edit my website myself?

Yes, for what changes often: copy, photos, news, products. We choose the tool based on what you want to manage yourself, and we show you how.

For the animated or interactive parts, we remain available to develop them further.

What technology do you use?

Whatever fits your project: a custom-coded website when speed and animation matter, Framer for a showcase site you want to edit easily, Shopify for an online shop. We explain the choice before we start.

Are the website and domain name in my name?

Yes. The domain name, hosting and access are in your name. If you stop working with us, you keep your website.

I already have a website. Will I lose my rankings if I redo it?

No, if the redesign is prepared. We list the pages that already bring you visits and redirect every old address to the new one, so Google and your visitors don't land on missing pages.

How does the budget work?

The quote is drawn up in writing after the first conversation, detailing the pages, features and animations included, before anything starts. Hosting and the domain name are paid directly by you.
