What Wanderful takes care of

  • The technical auditSpeed, mobile display, pages Google cannot read, missing titles, indexing errors: we list what is holding you back, in order of importance.
  • Keyword researchThe words your customers actually type, in your region and in their own terms, not in your industry jargon.

Our approach

What your customers type→A page that answers→A website Google understands→A lasting position

We write for your customers before we write for Google. A page that really answers a question almost always ends up ranking well; a page stuffed with keywords much less so, and it convinces nobody.

We do not promise rankings. Nobody controls Google, and those who guarantee first place often take shortcuts that end up costing a lot. We commit to the work done, and we show you every month what it produces.

Frequently asked questions

How long before I see results?

The first technical fixes and the Google profile can have an effect within a few weeks. For solid rankings on the important searches, it usually takes several months, depending on the competition in your industry and region.

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No. Nobody can guarantee a ranking on Google, and you should be wary of anyone who promises one. We commit to the work done and show you every month how your rankings and visits are evolving.

SEO or Google Ads?

The two complement each other. Google Ads brings visits right away, as long as you pay. SEO takes longer, but the visits keep coming without paying for every click. Often the most effective approach is to start with Google Ads and build SEO alongside.

My website was not built by you. Is that a problem?

No. We work on most websites, whether they are built with WordPress, Wix, Webflow, Shopify or custom code. If a limitation of your platform prevents an important fix, we tell you and suggest a solution.

Do I have to write articles myself?

No, we write them. We simply ask you to review the substance, so that each piece is accurate and reflects the way you work.
