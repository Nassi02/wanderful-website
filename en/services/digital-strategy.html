Frequently asked questions

Why build a digital strategy before running ads?

So you know who you are talking to, with which message and on which channels, before you spend. Without a strategy you test at random and the budget goes into clicks that never become customers. With a clear plan, every franc spent on ads, content or your website has a measurable goal.

How long does building the strategy take?

Usually two to four weeks between the first discovery session and delivery of the plan, depending on the size of the company and how much data there is to analyse. The action plan then covers the first 90 days.

What exactly do you receive at the end?

A clear document with your positioning, your ideal customers, an analysis of your competitors, the channels to prioritise, a 90-day action plan and the indicators to track every month. You can implement it with us or with your own team.

Is this suitable for a small business or a startup?

Yes, and that is where it helps most: when the budget is limited, you need to know where to put it. For a small organisation the strategy stays short and concrete, focused on two or three priority actions.
