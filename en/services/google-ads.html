Is it right for your business?

Yes, absolutely

Your customers already search for what you do on Google

Accountant, garage, dentist, beauty salon, tradesperson, lawyer: if people type your trade followed by your town, your ads can appear in front of them from day one. This is where Google Ads works best.

Yes, with one prerequisite

You sell online and have a product catalogue

Your products can appear with a photo and price right in the search results. This requires an up-to-date product feed: we first check that your shop can provide one before recommending this type of campaign.

Not as a first step

You're launching an offer nobody knows about yet

If nobody is searching for what you offer yet, there are no searches to capture. Google Ads can help test messaging, but other channels will deliver better results at the start — and we'll tell you so.

We first assess whether Google Ads fits your business, your goals and your budget. If it isn't the right channel for you, we'll tell you in our first conversation, before any quote, and point you towards what will make more of a difference.

Recognise yourself in one of these situations? The easiest thing is to talk it through: one conversation is enough to know whether it's worth it for you.

Our approach

The search typed→The ad→The landing page→The enquiry received

A campaign doesn't stop at the ad. What determines the result is the whole chain: what the person types, the ad that answers them, the page they land on, and the enquiry that comes out of it — or doesn't. We work on all four links together, because one weak link cancels out the work on the other three.

It's also what guides our optimisation. Rather than making random changes, we look in the data for the exact point where the chain breaks: a keyword that brings visits but never an enquiry, an ad that's seen a lot but rarely clicked, a page that loses visitors before the form. And that's where we fix it.

Frequently asked questions

How does the budget work?

You stay in control: your ad spend is paid directly to Google with your own payment method, you set the cap, and you can change or pause it whenever you like. We take no commission on it.

Our fee, on the other hand, is an amount agreed in advance and independent of what you spend on ads: we have no interest in pushing you to spend more.

The quote is drawn up in writing after our first conversation, detailing exactly what's included, before anything starts. What it depends on:

Number of campaigns Existing account or new setup Lead generation or e-commerce Number of languages Geographic areas Conversion tracking to set up Reporting frequency
How long before we see results?

Ads can run from launch day: visibility is immediate. However, it usually takes a few weeks of data before we can say what's working and what needs cutting.

The first few days are mainly about weeding out searches that have nothing to do with your business. That's normal — and it's exactly the job.

Do I keep control of my account?

Yes. The Google Ads account is in your name, you own it and you keep access. If you stop working with us, you leave with the history, the campaigns and the data.

What's the difference with SEO?

An ad appears immediately, and stops the day you switch off the budget. SEO isn't paid per click, but takes months of work before you climb the rankings.

The two complement each other: ads cover the present, SEO builds the future. That's what the demo at the top of this page shows, and it's what our SEO & Visibility service is for.

Which campaign types do you use?

We choose based on your situation, and we explain why:

  • Search — your ads appear when someone types a search. This is the starting point in the vast majority of cases.
  • Shopping — for online shops: your products appear with a photo and price. Requires a product feed via Google Merchant Center.
  • Performance Max — a campaign that runs automatically across Search, YouTube, Gmail, Display and Discover. Useful once there's already conversion volume, much harder to read when you're starting out.
  • Remarketing — showing your ads again to people who have visited your website without contacting you.
How is conversion tracking set up?

We connect Google Ads to GA4 and use Google Tag Manager to set triggers on the actions that matter: form submissions, clicks on your phone number, completed orders.

If your website doesn't allow this tracking, we'll tell you before we start. Running campaigns without conversion tracking is spending blind: we'd rather sort that out first.

Do you use a particular tool?

Yes. We've built our own tool connected to the Google Ads API: it reads the performance of the accounts we manage and flags anything drifting — a campaign burning through its budget without conversions, a keyword whose cost is soaring, a disapproved ad.

It doesn't make decisions for us. It saves us the time we'd otherwise spend compiling spreadsheets, and that time goes back into your account.

What if Google Ads isn't right for my business?

We'll tell you in our first conversation, before any quote. It isn't the right channel for every business, and launching campaigns that can't be profitable serves neither you nor us.
