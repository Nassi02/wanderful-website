Is it right for your business?

Yes, absolutely

You know you should post, but never find the time

Your clients look you up on Instagram, Facebook or LinkedIn before calling you, but your last post is old. A monthly plan and one hour of filming are enough to stay present all year, without thinking about it every week.

Yes, with one prerequisite

Your image isn't clear yet

Without a sharp logo, colours or a defined tone, every post goes in a different direction. We then start by setting your visual identity, with our Branding & Design service, before producing content.

Not as a first step

Your clients don't use social media to find you

If your clients mostly find you by searching Google when they have a need, social media won't be your first lever. We'll tell you, and point you towards SEO or Google Ads.

We first look at where your clients are and what they expect from you. If social media isn't the right channel, we tell you in the first conversation, before any quote.

Recognise yourself in one of these situations? The easiest thing is to talk about it: one first conversation is enough to see what your social media could say.

Let's talk about your project

What Wanderful takes care of

  • The editorial lineMonthly themes, chosen from the questions your clients actually ask you, your key moments and what worked the month before.
  • Filming and photosA session at your place or on a job site, prepared in advance with a shot list, so we leave with enough for the whole month. When it fits better, some visuals are produced with AI.
  • Editing and visualsReels, carousels, stories and LinkedIn posts, each in the right format and in your brand colours.
  • The copyCaptions, hooks and calls to action written in your tone, so people recognise you without seeing the logo.
  • Approval and publishingEverything arrives in your client area with a preview of the feed. You approve or ask for a change, then posts go out on the planned dates.
  • Comments and messagesWe reply to everyday comments and pass on right away any message that looks like a client enquiry.

The accounts stay in your name: you keep full access, and all the content produced belongs to you.

How we work

  1. 01

    Scoping

    We look at your current accounts, those in your industry and the questions your clients ask you. From that we define your priority networks, your tone and the first themes.

  2. 02

    Filming

    One hour a month, prepared in advance: you know what will be filmed, and there's nothing to learn by heart.

  3. 03

    Approval

    The month's content arrives in your client area with a preview of the feed. You approve it in about fifteen minutes, then everything is scheduled.

  4. 04

    Report

    Every month: what was seen, what got reactions, the messages received, and what we change for the following month.

What we need from you: access to your accounts, one hour of filming a month, and your feedback on the calendar. The questions your clients ask you on the phone are often our best content ideas: write them down.

Our approach

Your clients' questions→Theme of the month→One shoot→Eight posts

We start from what your clients want to know, not from what's trending this week. A question that keeps coming up on the phone almost always makes good content: it reassures those still hesitating and saves you from repeating the same answer.

We prefer consistency to one-off stunts. An account that posts every week, even simply, inspires more trust than one that alternates a polished video with three months of silence.

We apply this method to our own social media, and it draws on the training of Nassima Rizzotti, founder of Wanderful, in marketing and data analysis at HEC Lausanne. See our approach in detail.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to appear on camera?

No. A lot of content works without a face: hands at work, before / after, details, behind the scenes. If you're comfortable on camera, a few sentences to the lens are enough, and we guide you during the shoot.

Which networks?

Wherever your clients are, and not necessarily everywhere. For a tradesman or a local shop, Instagram and Facebook are often enough; for a business that sells to other businesses, LinkedIn comes first. TikTok comes on top, when your audience is there.

How much of my time does it take?

Usually one hour of filming a month and about fifteen minutes to approve the calendar in your client area. Wanderful takes care of the rest.

Is anything published without my approval?

No. Every piece of content appears in your client area with a preview of the feed. You approve, comment or ask for a change before anything is published.

Do you use AI for content?

For some visuals and some videos, yes, when it's faster and the result is up to standard: you can see examples above. Content showing your work, your team and your clients is filmed at your place.

How does the budget work?

A monthly fee agreed in advance, set out in writing after the first conversation, detailing what's included. Any sponsored ad budget is separate and paid directly by you.
