Is it right for your business?

Yes, absolutely

You have something to show

A place, a product, a craft, a team: things that are easier to understand when you see them. A short video shows it in a few seconds, where your customers spend their time.

Yes, with one prerequisite

You want to run ads

A video ad works better when you know who it speaks to and where it will run. We prepare both together, with our Google Ads or Content & Social Media services.

Not as a first step

Your message is not clear yet

A beautiful video does not replace a clear message. If you do not yet know what to say or to whom, starting with branding or digital strategy will save you time and budget.

We always start from the goal: filling a calendar, launching a product, hiring. If video is not the right tool to get there, we tell you in the first conversation, before any quote.

Do you have a video idea in mind? The simplest thing is to talk: one first conversation is enough to find the format, length and style that suit you.

Let's talk about your project

What Wanderful takes care of

  • The concept and the scriptThe message, the tone and above all the first seconds, the ones that decide whether people stop or keep scrolling.
  • The storyboardEvery shot sketched and timed before anything is produced, so you can approve the video before it exists.
  • The footageFilming on site, your existing footage, AI-created visuals or animation: we choose with you what best serves the project and the budget.
  • The editRhythm, music, colour grading: a video people watch to the end.
  • Motion designAnimated titles, animated logo, figures on screen, subtitles: everything that makes the video readable, even with the sound off.
  • The variationsVertical for Reels and TikTok, square for the feed, horizontal for YouTube and your website, and short versions to test in ads.

You receive the final files in every format agreed, and the music is chosen so it can be used wherever the video will run.

How we work

  1. 01

    Concept

    We start from your goal and your audience to write the message, the script and the hook for the first seconds.

  2. 02

    Storyboard

    Every shot is sketched and timed. You approve it before production: no surprises in the edit.

  3. 03

    Production

    Filming, visuals or animation depending on the project, then editing, music, animated titles and subtitles.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Your feedback on a first version, the adjustments, then every format ready to post or to run as an ad.

What we need from you: a brief on your goal, access to the venue if we film at your place, and your feedback on the first version. Nobody knows better than you what should appear on screen.

Our approach

A message→A hook→Subtitles→The right format

We design the video for where it will be watched. In a feed, people decide in a second whether to stop: the opening, the text on screen and the format matter as much as image quality.

We use AI when it serves the project: to prepare, to create some visuals or to produce variations faster. The concept, the edit and the creative choices are still made by us, and we never use it to imitate a real person without their consent.

And we do not make a video to sit in a folder: each one is planned with its channel, its formats and, if needed, versions to test. See our approach in detail.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a video take?

Usually a few weeks between the brief and delivery, depending on filming, length and the number of versions. The schedule is set out in the quote before we start.

Do we always need a shoot?

No. Depending on the project, the video can be made with motion design, from your existing footage, with AI-created visuals, or with an on-site shoot. We advise you on what will give the best result for your budget.

How many revisions are possible?

The storyboard is approved before production, which avoids big surprises. Rounds of feedback on the edit are then set out in the quote.

Can you also run the video as an ad?

Yes. We can run and track it with our Google Ads and Content & Social Media services, and prepare versions to test.

How does the budget work?

Each video gets a written quote, detailing what is included.
