Is it right for your product?

Yes, absolutely

Your product is hard to explain in one sentence

Software, app, platform: when people need to see it working to understand it, one minute of video says more than a page of text.

Yes, with one prerequisite

You are launching a product or a feature

Video helps a launch, as long as the positioning is clear. If needed, we work on it together first, with our Digital strategy service.

Not as a first step

Your website barely gets any visitors yet

A video only works if people see it. If there are no visitors yet, bringing them in first, with Google Ads or SEO, is often more useful.

We start by understanding your product and the question your prospects are asking. If a video is not what will move them forward, we tell you in the first conversation, before any quote.

Does your product deserve to be better understood? The simplest thing is to talk: one first conversation is enough to find the right length, style and budget.

Our approach

The problem→The solution→How it works→Try it

We do not give a guided tour of every feature. A good explainer video shows a problem your customer recognises, then how your product solves it, in a few simple steps.

We animate simplified versions of your screens rather than exact screenshots. The video stays readable on a phone, and it ages more slowly as your interface changes.

We use AI when it serves the project, for example to explore style directions or produce variations. The script, the animation and the creative choices are still made by us. See our approach in detail.

Frequently asked questions

How long should an explainer video be?

Usually between 30 and 90 seconds. Long enough to show the problem, the solution and how it works, short enough to be watched to the end.

Our product changes often. Will the video date quickly?

We animate simplified versions of your screens rather than exact screenshots: the video stays accurate for longer, and one screen can be updated without redoing everything.

Do you do the voice-over?

Yes, we arrange it in the language of your choice, with subtitles. The voice is chosen with you based on the tone of the product and the budget.

How long does it take?

Usually a few weeks between the first conversation and delivery, depending on length, the number of screens to animate and languages. The schedule is set out in the quote.

How does the budget work?

Each video gets a written quote, detailing what is included.
