Is it right for your business?

Yes, absolutely

You repeat the same steps every week

Replying to enquiries, copying contacts into a spreadsheet, chasing a quote, compiling your monthly figures: as soon as a task always follows the same steps, it can run on its own. That's where automation saves the most.

Yes, with one prerequisite

Your information is scattered

Some contacts in your inbox, others in a notebook, quotes in a folder: you can't automate what has no fixed place. In that case we start by tidying up with you, before connecting anything.

Not as a first step

You still get very few enquiries

Automating five enquiries a month won't change your week. In that case, the real issue is getting more of them: visibility, website, ads. We'll tell you so, and point you towards what will have more impact.

We first look at what a typical week looks like for you. If automation won't clearly save you time, we tell you at the first conversation, before any quote.

How we work

  1. 01

    Mapping

    We go through a typical week with you: the recurring tasks, how long they take, the tools they pass through. From that we pick the three automations that would save you the most.

  2. 02

    We build the first one on real examples and write the reply templates with you. Nothing is sent to your customers before you approve it.

  3. 03

What we need from you: access to the tools involved, a few real examples of enquiries and replies, and an hour to describe your typical week. That last point is what lets us choose what is really worth automating.

Our approach

Repeated task→Clear rule→Automation→Your go-ahead

We automate what repeats, not what requires your judgement. Before building any automation, we turn the task into a simple rule: when this happens, do that. If the rule isn't clear, the automation won't be either.

AI steps in where it's useful: reading a free-form message, understanding what the person is asking, preparing a draft. For anything that must be one hundred percent reliable, such as a send-out, a date or an amount, we use fixed rules you can check.

We apply this method to ourselves first: our tool connected to the Google Ads API, the client space where our clients approve their content, and this website's assistant all work this way. It also comes from the training of Nassima Rizzotti, Wanderful's founder, in marketing and data analysis methods at HEC Lausanne. See our approach in detail.

Frequently asked questions

Could AI say the wrong thing to my customers?

No. Messages sent to your customers are based on templates you have read and approved. AI is used to read the enquiry, sort it and fill in those templates with the right information: it doesn't improvise.

For delicate cases, such as a complaint or an unusual request, the automation prepares a draft and submits it to you before anything is sent.

Do I need to change my tools?

No. We start from what you already use: your email, your calendar, your spreadsheets, your CRM or your website form. We only suggest a new tool if it replaces several manual steps, and we explain why.

Are my data and my customers' data protected?

Each automation only accesses the information it needs. The accounts are in your name, and where possible we prefer tools hosted in Europe or installed on a server you control.

If you handle sensitive data, health data for example, we take this into account from the mapping stage and tell you clearly what can be automated in line with data protection law, and what should not be.

What happens if an automation breaks down?

Every automation keeps a log of what it has done and sends an alert if there's an error. The enquiry concerned isn't lost: it stays visible and can be picked up by hand.

How does the budget work?

Setup is an amount agreed in advance, set out in writing after the first conversation, with a breakdown of what's included. Tool subscriptions are in your name and paid directly by you: we take no commission on them.
