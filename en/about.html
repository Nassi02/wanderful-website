01 — The story

Wanderful started while I was still studying.

I founded Wanderful Marketing in 2024, in Yvonand, during my Master's in marketing at HEC Lausanne.

Since then we have worked with companies across French-speaking Switzerland on real projects: brand identity, websites, Google and Meta campaigns, SEO and automation.

03 — How we work

Small team. Big commitment.

A small team around your project: the person you set the strategy with is the one who carries it out and answers to you for it.

Direct contact

You talk to the people actually working on your project, not to a salesperson. Your questions get an answer without going through three departments.

From strategy to execution

What we decide together is what ships. Nothing gets lost between the intention and the launch.

Decisions you can follow

You know what changed, why, and what came of it — including when the answer is “that did not work, we are stopping it”.

AI, where it genuinely helps

It does not replace thinking — it frees up time for it. In practice, we use it for three things.

Explore

Running through twenty creative angles for a campaign before keeping two worth testing.

Automate

Letting repetitive work run on its own — performance pulls, follow-ups, report formatting.

Analyse

Reading large volumes of data quickly to spot what is drifting — then deciding by hand.

Magic, yes.
But never by chance.
