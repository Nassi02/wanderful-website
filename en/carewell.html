The client

A platform putting flexibility back at the heart of care

The platform connects nurses, healthcare assistants (ASSC), social care workers (ASE), care aides and midwives with hospitals, clinics, care homes and home-care services across Switzerland. The principle: activate an institution’s internal pool first, and only open up the external talent pool when needed — all free of charge for institutions.

An ambitious young startup in a market under intense recruitment pressure, needing serious, measurable acquisition rather than scattered one-off actions.

The challenge

Two markets, nine professions, a startup budget

Three structural constraints to reconcile from day one:

Genuine bilingualism. French- and German-speaking Switzerland aren’t translated — they’re adapted. Professional vocabulary, institutions, search habits: everything differs.

Granularity. Nine healthcare professions, nine distinct search intents, each deserving its own targeting.

Frugality. Delivering results without costly SEO subscriptions, getting the most out of every advertising franc.

The goal wasn’t visibility for its own sake: it was to get real healthcare candidates signing up, at the lowest possible cost.
