We understand your reality

What’s holding back your listings.

Before the solutions, let’s talk about what you face on the ground.

Too few mandates coming in

The market here is not short of buyers, it is short of properties to sell. The issue is therefore not finding clients but convincing owners to entrust you with their sale.

Indistinguishable from the others

Website, brochure, pitch: from one agency to the next, everything looks alike. Nothing tells the owner why you rather than the firm next door.

Listings that create no desire

Wide-angle photos, empty rooms, grey light. A mediocre presentation lengthens the time to sell and pushes the price down.

A long cycle that is hard to follow

Months pass between the first contact and the signature. Without organised follow-up, lukewarm contacts are lost and nobody knows where the signed deals came from.

A business of trust, with no visible proof

People entrust a sale to a person, not to a brand. Yet faces, completed sales and testimonials are often missing from the website.

A presence limited to listings

Publishing your properties is not enough: it speaks only to buyers, never to sellers. And sellers are what is missing.

How we fix it

A real lead machine.

We activate the right channels to attract serious sellers and buyers.

Acquisition aimed at sellers

Pages and campaigns built for the owner who is wondering: valuation, timescales, fees, taxation. That is where mandates are won, not in the listings.

A valuation tool that starts the conversation

A well-designed online valuation turns curiosity into a conversation. It is the most effective entry point in the sector, provided a real call-back follows.

Visuals that hold the price

Careful photography, a video tour, clean floor plans, aerial shots where the property justifies them. A good presentation shortens the timescale and protects the negotiation.

Standing in your own area

Becoming the name that comes to mind in three or four municipalities beats a diffuse presence across the whole canton. We concentrate.

Content that answers the real questions

Price per square metre by municipality, the stages of a sale, transfer duties, which renovations count. That is what owners look for before contacting anyone.

Contacts tracked through to signature

Knowing which source produced which mandate, not merely how many forms arrived. With a cycle of several months, it is the only measurement that matters.

The context

What makes property different.

Property here has a particularity that reverses the usual priorities: demand exceeds supply. Looking for buyers is of little interest, they are already there and watching the portals. All the value lies on the side of owner-sellers, who are rare, who take their time, and who compare two or three people before signing a mandate.

That changes the nature of the work entirely. It is not about distributing listings, but about being present during the months when someone is wondering whether to sell, and bringing them useful answers before any sales approach. Online valuation, local content and personal reputation are worth more than the advertising budget.

The long cycle finally imposes a discipline of follow-up. A contact made in March may sign in October; with no record of where they came from, you end up crediting chance and cutting what was working. It is the sector where measurement is hardest and most necessary.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us.

Do you work for agencies or for independent brokers?

For both, but not in the same way. An agency needs a brand and a steady flow of mandates; an independent broker first sells their own reputation across a small territory. The latter is largely a matter of personal branding.

Doesn't an online valuation mostly attract the curious?

Partly, yes, and that is normal. The value lies elsewhere: it puts you in contact with someone six to twelve months before they sell, which is to say well before your competitors. What counts then is the quality of the follow-up, not the raw volume.

Should we invest in video for every property?

No. Video is justified for properties where the volume, the view or the grounds do not come across in photographs, and for those whose price makes it worthwhile. For a standard flat, good photos and a clear floor plan are plenty.

How long before the first mandates?

The realistic timescale is counted in months, because it follows the pace at which owners decide, not yours. The first qualified contacts, on the other hand, arrive far sooner, and they are what to watch at the start.
