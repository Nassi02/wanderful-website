The Lausanne market

Get found in Lausanne, not just online.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us.

How do I choose a digital marketing agency in Lausanne?

Choose an agency that measures the enquiries you get, not just clicks, that leaves you as the owner of your Google accounts, and that gives you a written quote before starting. That is how Wanderful Marketing works for SMEs and startups in Lausanne and across the canton of Vaud.

Where is Wanderful based, and how do meetings work?

The agency is based in Yvonand, in northern Vaud. Meetings and follow-up happen by video call, so things move fast with no travel, wherever you are in the canton. You always deal with the same person.

How much does a marketing agency in Lausanne cost?

The first 30-minute call is free and you receive a written quote before anything starts. As a guide: SME showcase website from CHF 3,500, Google Ads management CHF 690 then CHF 490 per month, SEO support CHF 690 per month.

How quickly can we get enquiries?

With Google Ads, the first enquiries can arrive within the first few weeks. SEO is slower, often several months, but it keeps bringing visits without paying for each click. Most of our clients combine the two.

Do you also work in English?

Yes. We work in French and English: Google Ads campaigns, site pages and content, adapted to each audience rather than simply translated. This helps international companies and startups in the Lake Geneva region.

Who is behind Wanderful Marketing?

Wanderful Marketing Sàrl was founded in 2024 by Nassima Rizzotti during her Master's at HEC Lausanne. The agency uses AI at every step, with an in-house tool connected to the Google Ads API, while strategy and review stay human.
