Frequently asked questions

What people ask us.

How much does a website cost for an SME in French-speaking Switzerland?

At Wanderful, a one-page site costs CHF 1'490, a complete SME showcase site starts at CHF 3'500 and a “signature” site with animations and 3D starts at CHF 6'900. The final price depends on the number of pages, languages and content to produce; it is set in writing before work starts. Full cost breakdown in our guide to website prices in Switzerland (in French).

What is included in the price?

Page structure, copywriting, custom design, the mobile version, SEO basics (titles, descriptions, sitemap, structured data) and enquiry tracking with Google Analytics. The domain name and hosting are in your name and paid directly by you.

How long does it take to build the site?

Usually two to four weeks for a one-page site, four to eight weeks for a complete showcase site. The timeline is set in writing with the quote.

What is the Female Founders programme?

A programme for women founders and entrepreneurs in French-speaking Switzerland, limited to 5 places per quarter: either free Google Ads setup with the first month of management, or 20% off the website build with payment in three instalments. In return, we publish a case study of your project together.

Do you only work in Lausanne?

No. Wanderful is based in Yvonand, near Yverdon-les-Bains, and works across French-speaking Switzerland: Lausanne, Neuchâtel, Yverdon, Fribourg, Geneva, Valais and the Bernese Jura, by video call.
