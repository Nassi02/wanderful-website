The Neuchâtel market

One canton, two realities.

The canton of Neuchâtel has the lakeshore on one side, with the city of Neuchâtel, Boudry and Marin, and the mountains on the other, with La Chaux-de-Fonds and Le Locle. Customers don't search the same way: people type “fiduciaire Neuchâtel” or “garage La Chaux-de-Fonds”, rarely “canton of Neuchâtel”. A good online presence therefore speaks about each town where you actually work.

On most local searches, online competition remains modest: many businesses have neither a complete Google profile nor a page that clearly describes their activity in the region. A few well-made decisions are often enough to move ahead. We start with what brings enquiries fastest, we measure every call and every form, then we reinforce what works.
