We understand your reality

What's holding back your patient base.

Let's start with your day-to-day.

Gaps in the diary

A cancellation the evening before, a slot left empty, and the week turns unprofitable. In a practice the schedule is the revenue: what goes unfilled is never recovered.

Invisible in local search

Someone looking for a practitioner types the profession and the town, then clicks one of the top three results on the map. If your listing is incomplete or poorly ranked, you do not exist at that moment.

Trust is decided before the call

People choose a care provider the way they choose who to trust with something personal. With no photo and no clear account of your approach, the visitor moves on to the next practice.

Few reviews, and mostly old ones

Google reviews count twice over: they reassure the patient and they lift the listing on the map. A practice with four reviews from three years ago starts at a real disadvantage.

No time to communicate

Between consultations and paperwork, nobody opens Instagram in the evening. That is normal — and exactly why this part has to be delegated and planned.

A field where you cannot say just anything

Promises of results, patient testimonials, before-and-after photos: the professional rules vary by profession and by canton. Communicating without knowing them is a real risk.

How we fix it

A presence that reassures.

The right levers for effective, ethical healthcare communication.

Local search first

A complete, up-to-date Google listing with opening hours, photos, services and catchment area, plus pages built for searches of the profession-plus-town kind. It is the most profitable lever in the sector.

A site that books the appointment

Clear pages, an introduction to the team, fees and coverage, and above all online booking that works from a phone, without a call.

An organised way of collecting reviews

A precise moment in the patient journey, a simple message, and a systematic reply to every review received. Volume counts; consistency counts more.

Content that explains your care

Explaining a course of treatment, how long it takes, what is reimbursed: that is what people search for, and it is what establishes your expertise before the first consultation.

Local advertising that stays within the rules

Campaigns limited to your catchment area, on precise intentions, with every message reviewed against the rules of your profession.

Fewer cancellations

Appointment reminders, follow-up with patients you have lost sight of, practical information before the visit. A cancellation avoided is worth a new patient, for far less.

The context

What makes healthcare different.

Healthcare is not a sector like any other for marketing, for one simple reason: the demand already exists. Nobody needs convincing that they require a dentist or a physiotherapist. The work is therefore not to create a need, but to be the one who is found and chosen at the precise moment that need appears. This is why local search and reviews weigh more heavily here than anywhere else, and why social media comes second.

The second particularity is the framework. Whether you are a doctor, a therapist, a care home or a clinic, what you are allowed to write changes. We work with that constraint rather than against it: a sober, factual and educational tone inspires more confidence than a commercial promise in this field anyway.

Finally, many institutions in French-speaking Switzerland are not looking for patients but for staff. The shortage of care workers makes recruitment the real subject for care homes, clinics and home-care services. The methods are the same as for winning clients, applied to a different audience — and it is the ground on which we have the most experience.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us.

Am I allowed to advertise my practice?

Yes, within the limits set by your profession and your canton. What is generally regulated is promises of results, comparison with colleagues and certain testimonials. Informing people about your services, your hours and your approach is not a problem. We review every message with you before publication, and we drop anything doubtful rather than chancing it.

How long before new patients start arriving?

The Google listing and reviews have an effect within weeks, because they act on people who are already searching. Ranking a website takes several months. Advertising is immediate but stops with the budget. In practice we start with what works fast, then build what lasts.

I work alone in my practice — is this for me?

Often more so than for a large organisation. An independent practitioner depends entirely on local visibility, and that is where the effort pays off most. Conversely, if your diary is already full six months ahead, we will tell you: this is not the moment.

What if what I need is to recruit?

That is the subject for many institutions here today, and the approach is very close: understand what candidates are looking for, build pages that speak to them, and buy visibility where they search. It is what we ran for Carewell, in French and in German.
